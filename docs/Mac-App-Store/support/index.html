What belongs here, and what does not.

Good reasons to write in

  • Something in the App Store edition looks wrong or crashes
  • Weather or location permission is not behaving
  • A reading seems off for your machine
  • You want a feature this edition does not have yet
  • General feedback on the interface

Covered elsewhere

  • Fan control and the privileged helper belong to the direct download build
  • Privacy questions are answered in the privacy policy
  • Screenshots and edition scope live on the edition page
