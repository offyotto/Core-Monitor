Compare

How it compares.

Core-Monitor sits between broad menu bar suites and fan-only tools. Here's how it stacks up.

Vs. iStat Menus

iStat Menus covers more metrics. Core-Monitor focuses on thermals and power with a calmer UI and open-source code.

Vs. TG Pro

TG Pro is geared toward diagnostics and admin workflows. Core-Monitor is lighter, monitoring-first, with fan control available when you want it.

Vs. Macs Fan Control

Macs Fan Control focuses on fan speeds. Core-Monitor adds a full dashboard, menu bar status, thermals, battery, and power alongside fan control.

Vs. Stats

Stats is great for modular menu bar monitoring. Core-Monitor adds a full dashboard, fan control, and a stronger thermal focus.

brew install --cask offyotto/core-monitor/core-monitor

FAQ

Common questions.

What people usually want to know before installing.

What does Core-Monitor do?

It shows you live thermals, power draw, battery, memory, and fan speeds on your Apple Silicon Mac. Fan control is optional. Everything stays local.

Do I need the system helper?

No. Monitoring works without it. The helper is only needed if you want to control fan speeds.

Is it private?

Yes. All data stays on your Mac. No account, no telemetry, no analytics.

How does it compare to iStat Menus, TG Pro, or Stats?

Core-Monitor focuses on thermals and power with optional fan control. It's open source, free, and private. The Compare section above has specifics.

What doesn't it do?

It's not a cloud platform, not a fleet tool, and not trying to be the biggest stats suite. It's focused on heat, power, battery, and fans.

Where do I get it?

Download the DMG or ZIP from GitHub Releases, install via Homebrew, or get the read-only edition from the Mac App Store.
